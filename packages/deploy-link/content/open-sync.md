Open Sync pulls what is scattered across the services you already use — GitHub pull requests,
Gmail and Slack threads, Granola meetings — into one copy that lives on your own disk. See the
[README](https://github.com/massimoalbarello/open-sync#readme).

## Quick start

1. Click **Deploy on nibrun**. There is nothing to fill in.
2. Open the URL and connect the first account you want synced.
3. Leave it running; the copy fills in as it syncs.
