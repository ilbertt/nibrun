PDF Signer keeps your handwritten signature once and stamps it onto any PDF you give it, behind a
passkey — so the signature never travels to somebody else's server. See the
[README](https://github.com/massimoalbarello/pdf-signer#readme).

## Quick start

1. Click **Deploy on nibrun**. There is nothing to fill in.
2. Open the URL, register a passkey, and draw your signature once.
3. Drop in a PDF, place the signature, and download the signed copy.
