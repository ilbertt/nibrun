yarr — "yet another rss reader" — is a small, fast feed reader with a keyboard-driven UI and a
single file behind it. See the [README](https://github.com/nkanaev/yarr#readme).

## Quick start

1. Fill in `YARR_AUTH` on the deploy form as `username:password`. Until it is set, yarr serves
   your feeds to anyone who finds the URL.
2. Click **Deploy on nibrun**, open the URL and sign in.
3. Add a feed, and press `?` to see what the keyboard can do.
