GoatCounter counts page views without cookies and without following anyone between sites. See
[getting started](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/start) for what to do with the numbers.

## Quick start

1. Click **Deploy on nibrun**. There is nothing to fill in.
2. Open the URL. The first screen creates your site and your user — no command to run first.
3. Copy the counter snippet it gives you into the pages you want counted.
