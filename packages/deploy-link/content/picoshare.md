PicoShare is a minimalist file host: you upload, it gives you a short link, and whoever has the
link downloads it with no account and no preview page in the way. See the
[README](https://github.com/mtlynch/picoshare#readme).

## Quick start

1. Choose a passphrase on the deploy form — it is what you log in with.
2. Click **Deploy on nibrun**, open the URL and enter it.
3. Upload a file and share the link it gives you.

Uploads live on the volume, so they survive a redeploy. Set an expiry when you want them not to.
