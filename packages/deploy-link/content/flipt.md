Flipt is a feature flag server: define flags and segments in a UI, evaluate them from your
application over HTTP or gRPC. The [Flipt docs](https://docs.flipt.io/) cover the SDKs.

## Quick start

1. Click **Deploy on nibrun**. There is nothing to fill in.
2. Open the URL and create your first flag.
3. Evaluate it from your app against your own address.

It is the largest binary in this catalog, so the first request after a nap takes a moment longer
than most.
