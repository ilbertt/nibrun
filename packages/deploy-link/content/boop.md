Boop is a notification inbox of your own: your scripts and cron jobs POST to it, and the messages
collect somewhere you can read them. The [README](https://github.com/chrisgreg/boop#readme) shows
the request shape.

## Quick start

1. Choose an admin username and password on the deploy form.
2. Click **Deploy on nibrun**, then sign in with the pair you chose.
3. POST your first message from a script, and watch it land.

The links Boop sends out point at your own URL, so they work wherever you read them.
