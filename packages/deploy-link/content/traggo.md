Traggo is a tag-based time tracker: instead of picking a project from a list, you tag a span of
time however you like, and the dashboards are built from those tags. See
[traggo.net](https://traggo.net/).

## Quick start

1. Choose the first user's name and password on the deploy form — they create the account on boot.
2. Click **Deploy on nibrun**, open the URL and sign in.
3. Track your first span, then build a dashboard out of the tags you used.
