MicroBin is a small pastebin that also takes files and URLs, with expiring pastes, optional
encryption and QR codes. See the [README](https://github.com/szabodanika/microbin#readme).

## Quick start

1. Choose an admin username and password on the deploy form.
2. Click **Deploy on nibrun** and open the URL.
3. Paste some text or drop a file, pick an expiry, and share the link.

The links and QR codes it hands out carry your own address.
