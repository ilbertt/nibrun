nibrun-vitals shows you the machine it is running on: live CPU, memory, disk, network, visitors —
and the naps, because an app nobody has asked for in five minutes goes to sleep.

## Quick start

1. Click **Deploy on nibrun**. There is nothing to fill in.
2. Open the URL and boop the face.
3. Leave it alone for five minutes, come back, and watch it wake up.

It is the shortest way to see what a nibrun app actually is.
